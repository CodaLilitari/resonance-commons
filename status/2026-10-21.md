official sources where the receiver can do so. Upload custom code/results or a
demonstrated missing asset only. A prepared handoff is not a remote installation.

## Local q17 music integration

The same licensed q17 source is a **12-second, stereo, 44.1 kHz PCM16 WAV**.
Fresh Signal Core 0.1.3 public verification and analysis each exited 0, and
Music Geometry 0.1.1 completed through the persistent local dispatcher with
exit 0. Historical Voice Gate output for that exact source was reused unchanged;
it was not rerun for this integration. Geometry reproduced the earlier pilot's
measurements, which establishes repeatability here rather than independent
acoustic ground truth.

The tools retain different signal bases: Signal Core measures decoded/resampled
48 kHz PCM16 channels, Geometry uses 22.05 kHz channel-preserving PCM S32LE and
derived features, and Voice Gate uses per-channel 16 kHz model inputs. Their
peaks and scores must not be treated as interchangeable original-source values.
Tool times are relative to the 0–12 second clip; the separate curator mapping
places it at 10–22 seconds of the decoded parent source, not a verified master
timecode.

A custom **23-file, 2,929,264-byte evidence kit** has been prepared and
independently checked locally. Its SHA-256 is
`7c8726134237bbb4218109afb55745877540e3328c6904293af37b2fc5d5521e`.
It contains data, plots and interpretation instructions, with no source audio,
models or executable worker. **The kit is unsent at this checkpoint:** no
receiver interpretation or usefulness result is established. This is local
integration evidence, not a musical-accuracy or native-listening score.

## Local speech and control results

The `whisper.cpp 1.9.5` / `base.en` check scored two public LJ Speech clauses:
one substitution (`alimentary` → `elementary`), zero deletions and zero
insertions across 37 reference words. Micro-WER is **1/37, about 2.70%**, and
one of the two normalized clips matched exactly. Training overlap is unknown.
This finite check is not an estimate of broad transcription or lyric accuracy.

The failures remain part of the record. A four-second 440 Hz tone produced
`(eerie music)`. Three-second silence produced `[BLANK_AUDIO]` with a raw
0–10,000 ms segment, correctly marked outside input bounds. A two-second silent
prefix preserved the speech words but produced nonuniform segment shifts;
it did not establish reliable onset or word alignment.

The unchanged optional Voice Gate was then run on those same five source files.
At threshold 0.5, both speech clauses selected frames (218/237 and 253/263),
while silence and tone selected none (0/94 and 0/125). The prefixed speech case
selected 216/300 frames and remains a separate timing diagnostic. The four
speech/no-speech fixtures therefore agreed with their expected presence/absence
distinction. No threshold was tuned and no ASR output was suppressed or rescored.

Lilitari and Muse each returned a source-aligned interpretation of the disclosed
local records. Those interpretations do not count as hosted inference.

## Reading the outputs

Keep execution status, transcript quality, numeric timing bounds and semantic
interpretation separate. An out-of-bounds segment stays visible as raw diagnostic
output; do not silently clamp it or display it as a valid timed span. An in-bounds
segment can still contain an incorrect hypothesis, as the tone control shows.
Voice Gate scores are optional, uncalibrated supporting evidence, never a
mandatory lyric filter.

The machine-readable companion is [2026-10-21.json](2026-10-21.json). Private
conversation references, account details, source paths, runtime files and model
weights are excluded. This is a dated project report, not a claim of independently
reproduced public validation or five completed native audio services.
