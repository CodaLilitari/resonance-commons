## Two cases, with corrections counted honestly

Both cases use twelve-second stereo clips. Each route received the same packet for that case and was asked to distinguish measurements, algorithmic estimates and cautious interpretation. The evaluation criteria were fixed before the original responses. No source audio or executable audio worker was included, and these tasks required no receiving-host audio inference.

| Case | Supplied evidence | Lily / ChatGPT 6 Pro original | Muse original | Muse assisted outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| q17 | Measurements and plots covering temporal activity, spectral texture and channel/mono relationships | Accepted | Failed | v3 accepted after **two** assisted revisions; v2 remained below the mandatory criteria |
| q93 | Geometry JSON/CSV only; full twelve-second context, with attention on seconds 8–12 | Accepted | Failed | v2 accepted after **one** assisted revision |

Each accepted final response passed all six mandatory checks and earned the full usefulness score for all three requested observations. A revision is a continuation of its original case, not another independent trial. The record is therefore **two source cases, four original route responses, two original passes and two original failures**, with the two failed cases subsequently accepted after three assisted revisions in total. All proposed follow-up intervals remain unexecuted.

The corrections matter. On q17, review caught inaccurate timing, an inapplicable explanation involving channel cancellation, and unsupported musical or perceptual conclusions. On q93, it caught an incomplete beat-candidate spacing range and claims that low spectral flatness established tonal purity or that a pitch heuristic verified physical periodicity. The repaired responses retain useful comparisons while narrowing those claims to what the measurements support.

The q93 case also makes a useful limit visible: a higher average over the final four seconds can coexist with a lower value at the end. A global difference between channels does not establish their balance at each moment. Missing beat candidates do not prove that rhythm stopped. The accepted readings preserve these distinctions.

Packet sizes refer to the ZIP files, not expanded contents: **q17 is 2,929,264 bytes with 23 members**; **q93 is 36,460 bytes with 12 members**. These packets and their review records are distinct from the unchanged public source release. Acceptance concerns faithful and useful interpretation of supplied evidence, not independently established acoustic or semantic truth.

## Execution safeguards: local checks and a hosted stop

The command guard met the expected outcomes in **six local checks**: three fixtures for normal completion, excess output and a child retaining an output pipe, plus three instrumented regressions covering late observations, late result recording and exhausted output-drain allowance. Some checks deliberately provoke failure; success means the guard records that failure correctly and respects its cleanup conditions. These are local Linux results, not six hosted tests or six audio tests.

On the separate ChatGPT 6 Pro route, the next hosted output-cap check **stopped before its fixture launched**. The available documented execution control could return while a process kept running; the request required an enforced termination timeout. The returned evidence preserves the exact supplied sources and reports **zero fixture launches**. No fresh ABI/resource sample was taken, and the cap behavior remains **not evaluated on that host**.

An earlier, narrowly bounded hosted process-API diagnostic on that same ChatGPT 6 Pro route was accepted. It supports only the lifecycle behavior exercised there. It does not complete installation, erase the earlier bootstrap cleanup failure, or establish audio capability on that route. The prepared GPT-6 Voice Gate acquisition package remains **frozen, unsent and not ready for dispatch**; it still needs a verified hosted bootstrap and a separately reviewed update before use.
