## What the music interpretation trial showed

The q17 kit carries results for one licensed **12-second stereo clip**, with
data, plots and interpretation instructions. Its **23-member ZIP is 2,929,264
bytes** (22 payload members plus the manifest; 3,368,579 expanded
bytes), SHA-256
`7c8726134237bbb4218109afb55745877540e3328c6904293af37b2fc5d5521e`.
It contains no source audio, models or executable audio worker. Muse and the
separate GPT-6 Pro conversation received that exact custom kit; this tested
interpretation of evidence, not native
listening or receiving-host audio inference.

Muse's first response failed the frozen rubric: three of six mandatory gates
passed, and all three observations earned partial-usefulness scores. The
response included useful calculations but also incorrect beat times, an
invalid interchannel-cancellation explanation for magnitude-averaged features,
and claims about pulse, intention and perceptual mono equivalence that the
evidence did not establish.

The first assisted revision, v2, repaired the core relationships and earned
**2/2/2 for usefulness**, but passed only four of six mandatory gates. A second
assisted revision, **v3, resolved all six residual corrections and passed all
six gates**, retaining **2/2/2**. The corrected reading distinguishes hop
spacing from event duration, keeps aggregate chroma separate from the
one-channel pitch heuristic, and limits mono and silence claims to their
actual evidence. No further revision is needed for this bounded pilot.

This is **one original Muse model-case trial: the first response failed
(0/1), and v3 was accepted after two assisted revisions**. Revisions are not
new independent trials, and acceptance concerns useful interpretation of
supplied data and plots rather than independent acoustic truth.

Lily's separate GPT-6 Pro reading used the unchanged packet and request without
Muse's answers, corrections or the local evaluator checklist. Her original
response passed all six required gates, with three usefulness scores of 2/2.
The exact values and qualitative plot descriptions were independently checked.
Her proposed 4.5–6.5 second follow-up remains unexecuted.

There are therefore **two original model-route trials on one source clip**:
one passed its first response, and one failed initially and reached acceptance
after two assisted revisions. This is not a general success rate or a provider
ranking. A broader held-out set is still needed to assess reliability.

The local producer results remain as previously reported: fresh Signal Core
and Music Geometry runs completed; same-source historical Voice Gate output
was reused unchanged. Signal Core's 48 kHz PCM16 measurements, Geometry's
22.05 kHz transformed features and Voice Gate's per-channel 16 kHz inputs retain
their separate signal bases. Reproducing an earlier feature result establishes
repeatability here, not independent acoustic ground truth.

## Foundation progress and acquisition policy

Claude's returned foundation records were reconciled against the frozen source,
and both preserved response versions passed local canonical structure and
value-binding validation. Eight numeric claims matched their declared evidence,
time and channel basis. This acceptance is narrower than semantic truth or
independent proof of every reported remote filesystem event.

The acknowledged silence correction uses exact wording: **no run of 13 or more consecutive
fixed 20 ms frames meets the all-channel −45 dBFS RMS criterion**. The minimum
reported duration is 0.25 seconds, so the shortest qualifying run of full
frames is 0.26 seconds. This does not exclude shorter quiet runs or every
arbitrarily aligned interval with low aggregate RMS. A general silence
guarantee is not accepted. The correction is recorded separately; both returned
response files remain preserved unchanged, and acknowledgment ran no new code.

Persistence is dated evidence. The marker and installed-environment check was
performed on **October 20**. October 22 command records and a source recheck
support reuse of the foundation source/work files, with all 34 source records
unchanged. They do not establish a fresh October 22 check of the old installed
environment or indefinite persistence.

The foundation reconciliation is closed with those qualifications. The next
thin Voice Gate handoff remains prepared and unsent pending route availability
and the required fresh environment check.

The ChatGPT 6 Pro route first attempted the ordinary pinned public-source
download. Its partial return records failure before an HTTP response, so the
exact **99,106-byte source archive** was then submitted as a targeted fallback.
The earlier partial result and failure are preserved. The partial evidence
archive passed its 28-record manifest check, and three numeric bindings matched.
Its initial response failed canonical validation. After the source fallback,
the packet verifier and route check exited 0; unchanged v1 validation exited 2;
the separately corrected v2 passed, exit 0. An independent local run of the
frozen canonical validator also accepted the actual v2. Exactly six schema
values changed: the coordinator-attributed model label, signal coverage and
four claim identifiers. The three numeric claims and cautious interpretation
remained unchanged. All 30 returned-file manifest entries and 34 source records
were checked. The foundation is accepted within those limits.

The frozen handoff's four readiness gates still carry their original false
values. A separate local check using dated coordinator observations accepts
the four current access, evidence, model-selection and dedicated-conversation
gates. Its output identifies caller-supplied observations, not an independent
CLI attestation. Neither the label nor structural acceptance attests the provider's
backend, native hearing, indefinite file retention or general musical accuracy.

This follows the same delivery rule for every route: use ordinary official
acquisition first, then transfer custom material or a demonstrated missing
asset. An upload, a valid response schema and an accepted audio capability are
different milestones.
