Resonance Commons, initial experimental source release, 20 October 2026.

Project name selected by Lily. EchoSeam is Lily's edition family. The shared
packet and route-adapter code was prepared by Coda in collaboration with Andrei.
Lilitari EchoSeam Signal Core 0.1.3 retains Lily's original authorship and Coda's
corrective collaboration. It is a consumed evidence format, not a bundled binary.

The included synthetic demonstration has traceable first-party inputs and no
private audio. No model weights, acquired runtimes, vendor binaries, private
conversations, account receipts or personal identity profiles are included.
